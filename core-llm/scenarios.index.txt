DISAMBIGUATION — the confusable groups
- Accident (авария, ДТП, жол апаты, соқтығысу): happening NOW, at the scene, «только что / қазір ғана» → SC11. Happened EARLIER and the client is the VICTIM («в меня въехали», «я не виноват», «виновник застрахован у вас», «кінәлі … полисі сіздерде») → SC12. Happened earlier and the client claims under OWN CASCO («у меня каско», «КАСКО бар», own fault, yard / parking / hail / theft) → SC13.
- Claims: status or when is the payout → SC17. Refused, too little, disagree → SC19. Which documents, where to send → SC18. Book an inspection of the car → SC20.
- Payments: money charged but NO policy, payment failed → SC30. Policy issued and paid, but the document did not arrive → SC26. How to pay, installments → SC31. Certificate of payment → SC39.
- Buying vs owning: price of a NEW product → SC01 (OGPO) / SC03 (CASCO) / SC06 (travel) / SC07 (home) / SC08 (accident) / SC09 (individual DMS) / SC10 (a company). Issue OGPO now → SC02. Extend an EXISTING policy → SC27. Why my OGPO got more expensive, bonus-malus → SC32.
- Changes: add a driver → SC04. New car or plate → SC05. Phone, email, address → SC29. Sold the car, close the policy, refund → SC28.
- Health (ДМС): book a doctor → SC21. Is X covered → SC22. Which clinics → SC23. E-card missing → SC24. Buy DMS for myself → SC09. For employees → SC10.
- Documents: certificate, copy, duplicate → SC39. Policy not received after purchase → SC26. Documents for a claim → SC18.
- Contact: call me back later → SC36. Operator or human now → SC37. Rude staff, no callback → SC35. Suspicious call, SMS, link, request for a code → SC38.
- Abroad: planning a trip → SC06. Already abroad and sick or injured → SC15. Injured at home under an accident policy → SC16.
- Where: office and hours → SC33. Where inspections are done → SC20. Which clinics → SC23.
- Out of scope (loans, life insurance, weather, jobs) → SYS_OUT_OF_SCOPE. Vague, no concrete need → SYS_UNCLEAR.
